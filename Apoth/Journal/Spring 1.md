"You're the new witch?" I hear from the man before I see him. His tone paints a picture; a stern, intimidating, no-nonsense straight shooter. I couldn't tell you what I 'thought' that would look like, but its certainly not the fellow I turn around to greet. 

If I didn't know better, I'd say he was made of dough, a raw gingerbread man come to life. He is stout, pudgy, much like a bulldog. His form is all curves in contrast with the sharp angles of his attitude. His eyes are slitted, brow furrowed, face frozen in a permanent contortion of disdain. not my...dream first patient, but we don't get to choose these things. "Yes," I pipe back, refusing to drop my energy to his miserable level. 

"You don't look like you know what you're doing," he looks me up and down like I'm an artifact to be appraised. My mother was not a patient woman; I am my mother's daughter before anything else. 

"Same could be said for you," I sniped, eyes, darting pointedly at his bandaged hands. 

The ghost of a smile possesses his ace. "Touche," he says and beings to unwrap the mummy like bandages around his hands. "The old lady, the one before you, used to give me this salve. Burnt both hands to kingdom come as a wee lad. Palms fused to the hotcake pan. They fixed it for the most part, but the pain comes back every now and then. Think you could whip that up for me?"

"Nope," I answer. I turn my back, beginning to sweep basic supplies into a bag. 

"Excuse me?" I can hear him boil. I imagine him fluff up, like rising bread. 

"You're excused." I retort with a quick turn, beginning as if to move past him. 

"But!" he sidesteps to stand in my way. 

"Here's the deal," I thumb my pack straps "and be sure you share it with the rest of the village. I can't make you *that* salve because I'm not her. I'm sure she did things a certain way and you all had certain understandings. But she's not here now. I am. And I'm going to do my darndest to give you all what you need. I'll care for you just as much as she did, but I'll do it my way. Now, I'm off to gather reagents for something to help that burn. It probably won't be a slave and it definitely won't be what you're used to. If you don't mind," I move past him, gently but assertively brushing shoulders. 

I'm half out the door when he pipes up. 

"Eilok"

"What?" My cool, nonchalant facade flickers. 

"Names Eilok. I'm the village baker. And I think you're going to fit right in."
